What Curbside Kitchens is (and isn't)

Curbside Kitchens is a bulletin board for neighborhood meal-prep drops. Cooks post what they're making; neighbors reserve portions. That's the whole service. In particular:

  • We don't prepare, handle, package, or deliver food. Every meal is made by an independent home cook, not by us.
  • We don't process payments. Buyers pay cooks directly — Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, or cash. Any payment dispute is between buyer and cook.
  • We don't inspect kitchens or certify cooks. Reserving from a cook is your choice, like accepting a casserole from a neighbor.

If you cook

  • You're responsible for complying with the laws that apply to selling homemade food where you live, including Minnesota's cottage food law and food-safety rules. Our plain-English guide is a starting point, not legal advice.
  • Describe your food honestly — ingredients, allergens, portions, and price.
  • Honor the reservations you accept, or cancel them promptly so neighbors can plan.

If you reserve

  • A reservation is a commitment to pick up and pay. Cancel in the app if your plans change so the portions go back to the pool.
  • Tell the cook about allergies in the order note — and use your own judgment about what you eat.
  • Pay the cook what you owe, at or before pickup.
