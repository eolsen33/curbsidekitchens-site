how it works

Four steps, zero apps-between-apps

No checkout, no delivery drivers, no fees stacked on your food. Just neighbors cooking for neighbors.

Browse the week's drops

See what cooks in your community are making this week — menus, portions, prices, and the pickup window.

Reserve your portions

Grab what you want before it sells out. Add a note for allergies or requests.

Pick up curbside

Swing by during the pickup window. Your containers are labeled and waiting.

Pay the cook directly

Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, or cash at pickup. The money goes straight to the person who cooked.

for cooks

Turn Sunday batch-cooking into a side hustle

  • Post a weekly drop: your menu, your prices, your pickup window.
  • Portions are counted for you — reservations stop when you sell out.
  • Orders, notes, and pickup status in one place.
  • Photos of your meals, because meals with photos sell faster.
You keep 100%. Curbside Kitchens never touches the money — neighbors pay you directly. Before you sell, read our plain-English guide to Florida's cottage food rules.
